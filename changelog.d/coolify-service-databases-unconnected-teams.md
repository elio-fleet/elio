### Added

- **Coolify: databases inside a service** (the PostgreSQL of n8n…) are followed: engine, state, listed with the backups of the server, under the *Databases* filter of the Coolify page, and critical when made public, like a standalone database. Their backups are not given by Coolify's API: the tab says to check them in Coolify.
- **Coolify: teams not connected**: the Coolify page lists the servers of the fleet running Coolify's proxy that no connection attaches (a team or instance not connected yet, or a server Elio did not recognise), with a link to add a connection.
- **Keys page: the last keys scan summed up** (by hand or planned): when, servers read and failed, keys outside the keyring, with a link to the task.
