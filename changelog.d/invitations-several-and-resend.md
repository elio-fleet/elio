### Added

- **Invite several people at once** in *Settings → Users*: up to 20 addresses separated by commas, with the same role and language; the addresses that cannot be invited are listed with the reason, the others are invited.
- **Resend an expired invitation in one click**: invitations not accepted in time are listed under the pending ones for 30 days, with their author and expiry; *Resend* emails a new link valid for 7 days (the old link stays invalid).

### Changed

- Invitations revoked or expired more than 30 days ago are deleted every day (accepted ones are kept).
