### Added

- **My account**: change your email, with the current password. When emails are configured, the new address receives a link (valid 24 hours) and the email changes once it is followed while signed in; the current address is warned, and the change can be cancelled until then. Without emails, it changes at once. Every change is logged in the activity ("Email changed").
- **My account**: "My temporary accesses" lists your keys added for a while on servers and not ended yet, each with its server, account and end.
