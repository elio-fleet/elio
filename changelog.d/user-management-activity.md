### Added

- **Settings → Users**: each account shows when it last did something in Elio, and its menu links to its actions in the Journal.
- **Ask for a new password**: an administrator can ask someone to choose a new password (an account that may have been exposed). Until a password different from the current one is chosen, every page leads to *My account*; the request is logged in the Journal.

### Changed

- **Settings → Users**: *Deactivate* and *Reactivate* are now at the same place, the actions menu of the row.
